WHAT'S NEW IN V3
----------------

1. FULLY WALKABLE 3D BUILDING WITH PHYSICS (new)
   A real 4-storey Dhaka walk-up beside the safehouse with:
   - Ground floor + 3 upper floors + accessible roof
   - A real staircase with 16 individual treads per storey AND a low-slope ramp
     collider so the player can actually walk up (no teleporting)
   - Two flats per floor with partition walls and their own hinged doors
   - Doors and windows that OPEN and CLOSE with animation when you press E or tap
   - Full static-body collision on walls, slabs, treads, railings and parapet
   - Warm interior lights, roof parapet, water tank, cornice dentils and a jali
     lattice screen at the entrance (researched from Old Dhaka architecture)
   - Reach it in-game: it stands on a cleared plot 112 m east+south of the safehouse

2. CONVERSATION QUESTION OVERLAP FIXED
   The dialogue box was only 460 px tall while a long question bank needed more.
   It is now 630 px with a SCROLLABLE options list and a pinned input row, so
   questions can never overlap the typing box or the stop-talking button.

3. BENGALI SPEECH — VERIFIED + A TEST BUTTON
   1,509 recorded Bengali clips are bundled. Engine verification printed:
     key exists = true, player bound = true, clip decodes = 3.31 s, master = 0 dB
   In the phone: Settings > "বাংলা কণ্ঠ পরীক্ষা করুন" plays a test line.
   If you still hear nothing, the issue is the device/system audio output or the
   browser's autoplay policy (Web requires one click before audio can play).

4. CLEARER STORY / MISSION JOURNAL
   Phone > মিশন now shows: chapter number and name, place subtitle, the current
   objective, live progress (counter/need), every mission with its brief and
   reward, and what unlocks next.

5. RESEARCH-DRIVEN ARCHITECTURE DATA
   DhakaDistricts.gd now also contains ARCHITECTURE notes from Banglapedia, The
   Daily Star, DOUET and Hidden Heritage: Mughal cusped arches/jalis/courtyards,
   colonial semi-circular arches with keystones, pediments, cast-iron railings,
   vernacular walk-ups with chajjas and floor-defining cornices, and Old Dhaka
   narrow-deep courtyard plots.

6. CARRIED OVER FROM V2
   - Silent city (no street/vehicle/siren loops)
   - Authentic GLB rickshaw, CNG and bus models
   - Hailable rickshaw/CNG rides with BRTA fare payment
   - 23 levels including Kajla, Sontek, Jurain and Jatrabari districts
   - Mouse-click E interaction, on-screen Talk button, exam Next/End buttons
   - Brighter sky, more building colour, improved procedural faces

OPEN / CC0 3D ASSETS ADDED
--------------------------
184 real 3D models were downloaded from public-domain (CC0-1.0) sources and baked
into single-draw-call vertex-coloured GLBs in assets/models/cc0_merged/:

  1. kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly  (129 models: houses, walk-ups, shops, hospital,
     school, market, factory, roads, street furniture, trees, bicycles)
     https://github.com/kayquer/kit-cidade-lowpoly          License: CC0-1.0

  2. kayquer/scitizia-pessoas-64 (55 low-poly people, ~1.7 m tall, textured)
     https://github.com/kayquer/scitizia-pessoas-64         License: CC0-1.0

  3. NoLimitNexus/game-models   (9 animated humanoid rigs, Quaternius/KayKit)
     https://github.com/NoLimitNexus/game-models             License: CC0

Provenance: assets/models/cc0_merged/manifest.json, plus LICENSE.txt files in
assets/models/cc0_citykit and assets/models/cc0_characters.

How they are used:
  - Real modelled buildings replace a share of the procedural boxes in the city
  - Real modelled people stand around the safehouse square (better anatomy)
  - Real benches, bins, hydrants, cisterns, trees and bicycles line the lanes
  - All are ONE draw call each, so the weak GPU still runs ~40 FPS

CONTROLS
--------
WASD move | Shift sprint | Space jump | Mouse look | E / click talk-use
1-4 exam answer | Enter/E next | Tab/M/Esc phone
Walk up to a door or window and press E to animate it open/closed.
Climb the stairwell in the walkable building to visit any flat or the roof.
